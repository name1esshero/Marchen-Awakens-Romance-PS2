# Japanese environment texture text audit

## Scope and method

This read-only review covers the 189 Japanese baseline TGAs in `graphics/environments/` and their corresponding records in `graphics/index.json`. The audit authenticated each full-file SHA-256, filename and dimensions against the index; checked the TGA header and exact payload length; then visually reviewed all 189 exports in seven alpha-composited contact sheets, with enlarged nearest-neighbor views for text-like candidates. Byte-identical files are listed individually and point to the first reviewed identical row.

All 189 files are uncompressed true-color TGA (image type 2), 32 bits per pixel, top-origin descriptor `0x28`, with exact `18 + width × height × 4` byte length. The set totals 12,217,674 bytes and contains 157 distinct full-file hashes. All 189 images match their index names, dimensions, and hashes. No Japanese source was changed and no English override was created.

## Findings

Confirmed Japanese-bearing art appears in five files: the `ランダム` (“Random”) wordmark at row 074; Japanese marks on the gate/select artwork at row 089; the Japanese field-name/dice-operation atlas at row 111; mixed Japanese and English setup text at row 138; and a Japanese-styled wordmark whose text remains unread at row 157. Rows 002 and 003 contain glyph-like door/pillar emblems that may be symbols; their semantic status is unresolved and they are not counted as confirmed text. Several other assets contain English-only labels; the per-file inventory records them.

Bounds recorded below are raster-coordinate bounds measured or estimated from the exported image. They do not establish game UVs, draw placement, or runtime visibility. This audit does not establish that any candidate is rendered in a particular screen, nor does it validate a localized replacement in an ISO or emulator.
