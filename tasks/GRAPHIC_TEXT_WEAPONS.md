# Weapon texture lettering audit

## Scope and method

This read-only visual/catalogue review covers all 300 `graphics/weapons/*_jp.tga` baselines. Ten numbered contact sheets (30 images per sheet) were reviewed on a checkerboard; likely lettering was inspected at native or nearest-neighbor enlarged scale. Every image is an uncompressed 32-bit TGA (type 2, top-origin descriptor `0x28`). For all 300, filename, dimensions and complete TGA SHA-256 match the corresponding `graphics/index.json` image record. The 300 files contain 71 distinct full-file hashes; byte-identical repeats below inherit the first matching row’s visual finding.

Coordinates are approximate source-image pixel bounds, origin at upper left, half-open ranges `[x0,x1) × [y0,y1)`. They mark visible texture content, not recovered UV rectangles. Transcriptions are visual readings, not font/codepoint or runtime lookup evidence.

## Findings

One asset is a confirmed Japanese text surface: `00039_00921_0004_arrm_jp.tga` (#233), visually reading `ARMを装着する` (approximately “Equip ARM”). Two `armstprts` sheets (#264 and #300) show English `SLOT 1/2/3` labels followed by short color-coded labels whose script and reading are not confidently identifiable; retain them as localization-review candidates. English-only lettering also appears as `GET` (#241) and a stylized `ARM START` mark (#265). The special-control atlas (#252) contains Latin/digit control labels `R1`, `R2`, `L1`, `L2` and language-neutral controller/arrows. The other reviewed images show textures, UI borders, masks, symbols, element icons, character/weapon art, or effects without readable words.
